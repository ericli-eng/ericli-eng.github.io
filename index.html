ASME ROBOTICS

Rover system design.

The rover CAD captures system-level integration: a modular frame, multi-wheel mobility, articulated mechanical linkages, electronics packaging, and an upper mechanism integrated into one platform.

Designing the whole system—not just a part.

These assembly views show how mechanical, electrical, and structural decisions interact. The rover project is a strong example of designing around interfaces: where mechanisms mount, how loads move through the frame, where electronics live, and how subsystems remain serviceable.

  • Modular aluminum-extrusion chassis
  • Multi-wheel articulated suspension / linkage geometry
  • Integrated drive and actuator components
  • Electronics and control hardware packaging
  • Assembly-level CAD and subsystem iteration

Comparing Performance of Mamba and Self-Attention Models on Imbalanced Image Classification Tasks

The study compares VIM-TINY (Vision Mamba) and DEIT-TINY (Vision Transformer) across balanced and artificially imbalanced CIFAR-10/CIFAR-100 datasets, then extends the comparison to selected MedMNIST2D datasets.

6 / 6baseline CIFAR settings where VIM-TINY outperformed DEIT-TINY
4 / 5MedMNIST datasets where VIM-TINY beat baseline DEIT-TINY
300training epochs used for the model experiments

Key takeaway: the results suggest Mamba is comparatively robust under imbalance, while stronger augmentation is especially important for making the Transformer baseline competitive.
